# Duel disk model

The duel disk model and its textures (`disk/duel_disk.json`, `textures/disk/duel_disk*.png`) are based on
"duel disk from Yu-Gi-Oh Duel Monsters (blocky)" by burning-icecream
(https://sketchfab.com/burning-icecream), published on Sketchfab at
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/duel-disk-from-yu-gi-oh-duel-monsters-blocky-14d4cc46e53a46ad903a9aa5f64e681c
under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).

Changes: placed on the player's left arm and scaled, textures packed into one atlas, straps added, a glowing layer
for the card zones, recolored disk skins, and a standby pose with deploy and fold animations.

mod is GPL-3.0.
